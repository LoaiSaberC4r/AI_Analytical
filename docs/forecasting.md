# Comparisons and forecast

Comparisons support `arrivals` and `averageOccupancy`. Use `previousDuration`, `previousLocalDay` or `previousLocalWeek`. Both windows use half-open bounds. A local alignment preserves wall-clock boundaries. For repeated local times it prefers the original offset when available and otherwise the earlier occurrence. A skipped boundary or unequal elapsed duration returns a reason and unavailable deltas. A zero previous value still permits an absolute difference. A percentage difference requires a qualified nonzero previous value. Video comparison uses the previous equal duration in the same explicit loop.

`GET /api/v1/analytics/forecast?zoneId=<guid>&siteId=<guid>` reads clean live zone hourly rollups and predicts weighted zone occupancy. An eligible history hour has a measured average, at least the configured coverage, and no flagged gap. Forecasts need at least 28 distinct usable site-local dates and four matching weekday/hour observations for each target. The default lookback is 56 days. Missing or dirty hours reduce the sample count. The forecast reports actual training bounds and percent of lookback hours that qualified.

The model takes the median for each matching hour. The P25–P75 band describes historical variability. Target UTC instants include offset-aware local labels. The clock skips nonexistent local hours and includes both occurrences of repeated hours. History contributes at most one sample per local date and hour, choosing the earlier eligible occurrence on a repeated hour. For time zones where UTC rollups do not align to whole local hours, that slot returns insufficient data. Insufficient days or sample counts return a reason and null values, including when there are zero qualifying buckets.
